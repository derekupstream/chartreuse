# Getting Started with Trutlyse

This guide walks you through creating your first project on Trutlyse — from signing up to sharing your impact report.

## Create your account

When you sign up, you'll need three things:

- **Email**
- **Password**
- **Organization name**

Your organization is the workspace your team shares. You'll be able to invite members to your organization later — that's why setting up the org up front matters, even if you're starting solo.

## Start a project

Once your account is ready, you have two ways to begin:

- **Start a custom project** — build from scratch with your own data.
- **Start from a template** — open a project that's already pre-filled with the typical inputs for a venue type.

We recommend starting from a template if one fits your operation. Many of the single-use, reusable, dishwashing, and logistical elements are already filled in for you, so you spend less time on setup and more time on results.

> **Want a template built for your operation?** One of the ways we work with our partners is by building custom templates with them. Email us if you're interested in creating your own template.

## Choose actuals or projections

Every project on Trutlyse is one of two types:

- **Actuals** — track real reuse data after a program has run. Currently, actuals are supported for **events** only.
- **Projections** — model the impact of a reuse program before or while you run it.

If you're creating a Projections project, pick the category that best matches your venue:

- Cafeteria
- Coffee shop
- Fast casual restaurant
- Food hall stand
- Or create your own category

## Set your location and utility rates

Enter where your project is located, then choose your utility rates. You can either:

- **Search by state** to use the rates we have on file, or
- **Enter custom rates** if you have specific numbers from your utility provider.

## Enter your project data

If you started from a template, most of this will already be filled in — review and adjust as needed. If you're building a custom project, you'll work through these sections in order:

### Single-use purchasing
Choose the items you currently buy from our product database. **Want a product added that isn't there yet?** Email us and we'll help get it into the database.

### Reusable containers
Add the reusables you'll be using to replace single-use items in your reuse system.

### Dishwashing
Enter your dishwashing information — equipment, cycles, and any related details.

### Additional costs
Add any other costs that affect your reuse program. We support a wide range, including:

- Labor
- Waste hauling
- Equipment
- Renovations

## View, edit, and share your dashboard

After you've finished entering your data, head back to the dashboard. You'll see the **economic and environmental impact** of your reuse system.

From here you can:

- **Edit** — click the Edit button to change the dashboard's name and description.
- **Print** — click Print to print out the dashboard as a report.
- **Share** — click Share to generate a short link you can send through email, text, or social media.
